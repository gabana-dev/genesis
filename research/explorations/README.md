# Explorations

Investigations into how Genesis should think, not what Genesis should believe. Not the
Canon, not a hypothesis about the world Genesis studies — an inquiry into the research
process itself.

An exploration might ask what makes a hypothesis good, what makes a question well-formed,
or how a method should work — improving the instruments of research rather than using
them. If a hypothesis investigates a claim about adaptive knowing, an exploration
investigates the practice of investigating.

## Format

One file per exploration: `short-slug.md`. No fixed structure is imposed — explorations
are freer than hypotheses, since they're examining method rather than making a testable
claim about the world. Most will end with open questions and a current conclusion, held
provisionally.
