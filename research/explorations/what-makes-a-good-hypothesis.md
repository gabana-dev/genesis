## Why this exploration exists

Genesis depends on hypotheses to improve understanding.

The quality of the research program depends directly on the quality of the questions it asks.

Before producing many hypotheses, it is worth investigating what makes one genuinely valuable.

This document explores that question.

---

## A good hypothesis is not a belief

A hypothesis is not something we hope is true.

It is something we are genuinely willing to discover is false.

A hypothesis exists to expose an idea to reality.

---

## A good hypothesis reduces uncertainty

The purpose of a hypothesis is not to accumulate facts.

Its purpose is to reduce uncertainty about an important question.

The value of a hypothesis depends on how much understanding would improve if the answer became known.

---

## A good hypothesis has clear scope

A hypothesis should investigate one question.

When a hypothesis grows large enough to answer multiple questions simultaneously, it becomes difficult to test and difficult to revise.

Scope protects inquiry.

---

## A good hypothesis can be challenged

Every hypothesis should invite disagreement.

A useful question is:

"What observation would convince us we are wrong?"

If no answer exists, the hypothesis is not yet scientific enough.

---

## A good hypothesis produces consequences

A good hypothesis changes what we would do.

If every possible outcome leaves our thinking unchanged, the hypothesis has little value.

Research should influence future decisions.

---

## A good hypothesis creates better questions

Sometimes the greatest value of a hypothesis is not the answer it produces, but the better questions it reveals.

Good hypotheses increase the quality of future inquiry.

---

## Open questions

- Can the quality of a hypothesis be evaluated before any experiment?
- What makes one question more valuable than another?
- Can uncertainty itself be measured?
- Are there different classes of hypotheses within Genesis?

---

## Current conclusion

Genesis should evaluate hypotheses not by how impressive they sound, but by how effectively they improve understanding.

The quality of the research program depends on the quality of the questions it chooses to investigate.
