## 6. Standing practice adopted today

- **Salvage register** ([`SALVAGE-REGISTER.md`](SALVAGE-REGISTER.md)) — when a contract closes,
  separate the *use* that failed from the *finding* that survived. A weak instrument usually
  fails a completeness claim while remaining valid for a presence claim.
- **Baseline hierarchy** ([`next-phase-review-2026-08-19.md`](next-phase-review-2026-08-19.md) §8)
  — every future contract names a competent alternative, not only a luck null. Tier 4, Genesis
  with its epistemic machinery disabled, has never been used and is the only thing that could
  substantiate the architecture's value.
- **Check plausible premises, not just surprising results.** Today's largest error survived a full
  day because "21 days" did not sound wrong. The rule "a surprising number is a suspected bug"
  was applied to zero `withdrawable` and to a 500 KB parse failure, and never to a number nobody
  would blink at.
