# 0001 — Research-triage reframe

**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Status:** accepted
**Reversibility:** costly — reframes the project's self-conception.

## Context

A literature-reconciliation pass ([`../prior-art-and-opportunity-map.md`](../prior-art-and-opportunity-map.md))
established that Genesis's foundations — belief states, Bayesian/recursive updating,
observation-model learning, dynamic filtering, active sensing, causal/interventional
reasoning, closed-loop/model-based agency — are all established science. The from-scratch
derivations built internal coherence and the research pipeline, but did not produce new
science. An external review independently reached the same conclusion.

## Decision

1. **Genesis stops treating independent rediscovery as research.** (Canonized as the
   "Rediscovery Is Not Discovery" First Principle + mandatory research gate in
   [`../../canon/research-methodology.md`](../../canon/research-methodology.md).)
2. **The novel-cognitive-architecture thesis is acknowledged as established science** — not
   a Genesis discovery.
3. **The axiology / install thesis is acknowledged as an open philosophical problem** (the
   is/ought gap; the contested neo-Aristotelian bridge), not a tractable engineering problem.
4. **The Research OS is the strongest surviving candidate contribution** — but its novelty
   and value remain unvalidated.

## Reasoning

Measured against prior art, the grand architectural thesis does not survive, and axiology is
not Genesis's to solve. Continuing to climb the POMDP/RL capability ladder would spend
laboratories rediscovering known machinery. The honest move is to stop, name what is
established, and reserve effort for whatever (if anything) is genuinely open.

## What we gave up

> The claim that Genesis is deriving a novel mind from first principles.

## Still open — deliberately not decided here

Which project Genesis now is:

- **A. Methodology project** — validate the Research OS.
- **B. Normal research** — reflexive/performative decision-making (adjacent to markets; crowded; no special edge).
- **C. Modest philosophy** — the axiology/install problem (high-risk, likely non-resolving).
- **D. Completed learning vehicle** — acknowledge the understanding gained, wind the grand thesis down.

The direction is not selected in this record. The immediate next action (not a direction
commitment) is a prior-art audit of the Research OS itself, to determine whether it is
materially different from existing systems and could yield a measurable advantage.
