## Purpose

Genesis investigates adaptive intelligence.

As the research program grows, individual concepts begin to form relationships that are difficult to understand when viewed in isolation.

This document records those relationships.

It is not an ontology.

It is not a glossary.

It is not the canonical definition of any concept.

Instead, it serves as a living conceptual map that reveals how the major ideas within Genesis relate to one another.

Its purpose is discovery rather than definition.

As research progresses, new concepts may appear, existing relationships may change, and entirely new structures may emerge.

This document therefore remains intentionally provisional.

---

## Reading the Landscape

Relationships shown here are not claims of causation.

They represent the current understanding of how concepts appear to interact within the Genesis research program.

Some relationships are well understood.

Others remain hypotheses.

The landscape should become increasingly coherent through research rather than through design.

---

## Layer 1 — Reality

Everything begins with reality.

Genesis assumes that cognition does not create reality.

It develops through interaction with it.

```
Reality
│
├── Environment
├── Time
├── Change
├── Constraint
└── Observation
```

**Open questions:**

- What aspects of reality are directly observable?
- Which aspects can only be inferred?
- How should Genesis distinguish between reality and its internal models of reality?

---

## Layer 2 — Perception

Reality becomes available through observation.

Observation alone does not produce understanding.

Information must first be interpreted.

```
Observation
│
├── Information
├── Signal
├── Noise
├── Context
└── Attention
```

**Open questions:**

- What transforms observation into meaningful information?
- How should context influence interpretation?
- Can attention itself become adaptive?

        ▼

These concepts are defined elsewhere within the Canon.

This landscape records only their current relationships.

**Open questions:**

- Are intermediate concepts missing?
- Does understanding necessarily precede wisdom?

## Layer 6 — Intelligence

Genesis currently views intelligence as emerging from adaptive knowing rather than preceding it.

```
Adaptive Knowing
        │
        ▼
Intelligence
        │
        ├── Reasoning
        ├── Intuition
        ├── Judgment
        ├── Adaptation
        ├── Creativity
        ├── Restraint
        └── Wisdom
```

This remains an active area of investigation.

Genesis does not currently assume that intelligence is reducible to prediction, optimization, or computation alone.

**Open questions:**

- What makes intelligence adaptive?
- How should intuition relate to reasoning?
- Can wisdom emerge computationally?

## Layer 8 — Action

Knowing ultimately returns to reality through action.

```
Understanding
        │
        ▼
Decision
        │
        ▼
Action
        │
        ▼
Feedback
        │
        ▼
Reality
```

This completes the current epistemic cycle.

Reality shapes cognition.

Cognition shapes action.

Action generates new interaction with reality.

The cycle then begins again.

## Open Frontier

This document intentionally ends without conclusions.

Its purpose is to reveal the current conceptual terrain rather than close investigation.

Every addition should improve the coherence of the landscape rather than simply increase the number of concepts.

Genesis is not attempting to accumulate ideas.

It is attempting to discover the structure through which understanding itself emerges.
