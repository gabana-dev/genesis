**Kept because the failure is more instructive than the correction.**

Two errors compounded. The universe was four hours old, not the 21 days asserted across three
documents — "21 days" was `hl1`'s *declared duration* misread as elapsed time. And the supporting
statistic, "the top 300 of 5,395 wallets hold 97.8% of notional", was true but measured
concentration *within* the sample and was used to claim something about wallets the sample had
never seen.

**A statistic computed inside a sample says nothing about what the sample never saw.**
