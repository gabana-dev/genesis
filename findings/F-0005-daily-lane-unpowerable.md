GEN-1 could resolve nothing below a 54.67% hit rate and measured 52.10% — **the best result in
the experiment sat below the threshold the experiment could detect.** ECON-1's declared read is 90
independent days, which resolves nothing below 58.5%, so it returns a null in November whether or
not the effect is real, and K2 closes the directional programme on that null.

**What it does not mean.** It does not mean there is no edge. It means the project reported
"no edge" where the truthful statement was **"no edge larger than about five percentage points."**
Conclusions resting on measured magnitudes are unaffected — market making died on 0.00154 bps
against 5.19 bps of cost, which is a ratio, not a p-value.
