# Ontology

> **Type 1 · Research — scaffold.** The substance is authored by the researchers; Claude
> maintains form only (structure, formatting, links) per the
> [Governing Principle](../ai/collaboration.md#governing-principle). Status: empty scaffold —
> entry format provided, entries unwritten.

**Purpose:** The controlled vocabulary and conceptual model of Genesis — the entities the
project reasons about and how they relate. One authoritative definition per concept.
When a term is used across the repository, it means what this document says it means.

---

## Entry format

> Each concept gets one entry. Keep definitions load-bearing: precise enough to constrain
> use, not merely describe it.

```
### <Concept>

**Definition:** <what it is, precisely.>

**Distinguished from:** <the nearest concepts it must not be confused with.>

**Relations:** <how it connects to other entries — links.>

**Status:** proposed | working | stable

**Source:** <link to the research/decisions/ or research/journal/ entry where this
concept was reasoned. A definition with no traceable source does not belong in the canon.>
```

## Notes

- A concept enters this document only when the researchers decide it. AI may flag that a
  term is being used before it is defined, or used inconsistently — but does not supply
  the definition.
- Superseded definitions are not deleted silently; record the change and its reasoning in
  [`../research/decisions/`](../research/decisions/).
